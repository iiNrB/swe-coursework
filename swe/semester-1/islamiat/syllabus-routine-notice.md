# Islamiat — Syllabus, Routine & Notice

## Syllabus
Hum 4145 · Credit: 2.00

Tawheed: Taweedul Uluhia, Taweedul Rububia and Taweedul Asma-was-sifat,
Aqeedah/creeds of Islam: Creeds of Ahlus-sunnah-wal-jamah; Sources of
Islamic Code of Life; Social, Economic and Political system of Islam;
Islamic ethics and Moral values: Human values in Islam, Dignity Family
Ties; Role of Islam in eradicating social evils; Islam and the world peace.

**Recommended Texts:**
1. A. A. B. Philips, *The Fundamentals of Tawheed (Islamic Monotheism)*,
   2nd ed., International Islamic Publishing House, 2006

Source: [Academic Catalogue — CSE, IUT](../../academic-catalogue-cse-2017-18.pdf), p. 31
